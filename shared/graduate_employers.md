# UK Construction Graduate Employers — Reference

A non-exhaustive snapshot of major UK construction employers that run graduate schemes, placements, and internships. Application windows and details change year to year — always confirm against the employer's careers page before applying.

## Tier 1 Main Contractors

These typically run structured graduate schemes (rotational, 2 years, leading to chartered status sponsorship). Application windows usually open September–October for the following September start.

- **Mace** — strong London focus, offices/residential/aviation
- **Skanska** — broad sectors including healthcare and infrastructure
- **Sir Robert McAlpine** — long-established, premium projects
- **Bouygues UK** — large-scale public sector and residential
- **Wates** — public sector and large residential
- **Multiplex** — high-rise, central London, prestigious
- **Balfour Beatty** — broad civils/infrastructure as well as building
- **Kier** — public sector, infrastructure
- **Galliford Try** — broad sectors
- **Morgan Sindall** — fit-out and refurbishment strong
- **BAM Construct UK** — public sector, education, healthcare
- **Vinci Construction UK** — broad sectors
- **Laing O'Rourke** — engineering-led, MMC / DfMA leadership
- **Costain** — infrastructure, energy
- **Willmott Dixon** — public sector, education
- **ISG** — fit-out, retail, data centres
- **Bowmer & Kirkland** — distribution / logistics / industrial
- **John Sisk & Son** — diverse, private and public sector
- **JRL Group** — primarily groundworks and concrete frames
- **McLaughlin & Harvey** — Scotland/NI focused with UK reach

## Tier 2 / Regional Contractors

Often less structured grad schemes but excellent hands-on experience and faster progression. Less competition than tier 1.

- **Berkeley Group** (residential — multiple operating companies: St James, St George, St Edward, etc.)
- **Bellway** / **Barratt Homes** / **Persimmon** / **Taylor Wimpey** (housebuilders — different business model from main contractors)
- **United Living**
- **Beard Construction**
- **Speller Metcalfe**
- **TClarke** (M&E specialist)
- **Stepnell**
- **Gilbert-Ash**
- Regional family firms — every region has 5–10 established mid-size contractors worth knowing

## Specialist Sub-Contractors

For students interested in specific trades or technical roles:
- **Lakesmere** / **Prater** / **MITIE** (facades, M&E)

## Local Authorities & Public Sector

Sometimes overlooked but offer steady graduate progression:
- **Network Rail** — civils/rail focus, structured grad scheme
- **HS2** — large grad intake, infrastructure
- **Highways England** / **National Highways** — civils
- **TfL** — diverse engineering and PM roles
- **Local councils** — building control, planning, project management — often listed on local council websites

## Where to Find Their Schemes

For each employer above:
1. **Careers page** — go directly to `[company].com/careers/graduates` or similar. Tier 1s usually have dedicated grad recruitment pages.
2. **Prospects / TARGETjobs** — most listed there too.
3. **LinkedIn** — search "[Company] graduate scheme" and follow the page.
4. **Your university Careers Service** — many tier 1s partner with specific universities for early-access events and applications.

## Application Window Reality Check

- **Grad schemes (final-year students):** open Sep–Oct, close Dec–Feb (often earlier if they fill).
- **Year-in-industry placements (Year 3):** open Sep–Oct of the year before placement, close Jan–Mar.
- **Summer internships (any year):** open Nov–Jan for the following summer, close Feb–Apr.
- **Some firms have rolling intakes** — Berkeley Group, regional contractors, smaller consultancies. These post throughout the year.

## How to Differentiate Yourself

Tier 1 grad schemes are competitive (often 50:1 application-to-offer ratio). Things that genuinely move the needle:
- A relevant placement or summer internship (any contractor counts, not just tier 1)
- CSCS card already held (shows initiative)
- Society leadership role (CIOB Student Chapter, ICE Student Society, etc.)
- Specific connection to the firm — networking event, alumni contact, attended their CPD talk
- A site-based hobby — self-build with a charity, building/making things
- Strong predicted grade backed by individual module scores in relevant areas
