# UK Construction ATS Keyword Bank — Student Edition

Use this bank to (a) extract JD keywords and (b) ensure tailored CVs use the contractor's preferred terminology. Only use a keyword on the CV if the student genuinely has the underlying experience.

## Job Title Variants (recognise as relevant for students)

Graduate Construction Manager · Trainee Construction Manager · Construction Management Graduate Scheme · Graduate Site Manager · Assistant Site Manager (Graduate) · Year-in-Industry Placement · Industrial Placement · Summer Internship — Construction · Trainee Project Manager · Apprentice Construction Manager (Level 6 degree apprenticeship)

## Cards & Certifications (rare for students but valuable)

- CSCS — Green (Labourer/Visitor — basic), Red (Trainee), Blue (Skilled), Gold (Supervisor), Black (Manager — typically post-experience)
- CITB Health, Safety & Environment test (required for most CSCS cards)
- First Aid at Work (3-day) / Emergency First Aid at Work (1-day)
- IOSH Working Safely / IOSH Managing Safely
- SSSTS (Site Supervisors Safety Training Scheme) — useful but not expected of students
- SMSTS (Site Management Safety Training Scheme) — typically post-grad, but valuable if you have it
- Asbestos Awareness (UKATA)
- Mental Health First Aid

## Professional Bodies (Student Membership)

- CIOB Student Member (free)
- ICE Student Member (Civil Engineering)
- RICS Matrics Student Member
- APM Student Member (Project Management)
- Working towards MCIOB / MAPM / MRICS / MICE (post-graduation)

## Regulations & Standards (Awareness Level for Students)

Don't claim deep expertise — claim awareness:
- CDM 2015 (Construction Design and Management Regulations) — know the four duty-holders
- Building Safety Act 2022 — know it exists and applies to higher-risk buildings
- Building Regulations Part B (Fire), Part L (Energy), Part M (Access) — broad awareness
- BREEAM (Pass / Good / Very Good / Excellent / Outstanding)
- LEED · WELL Building Standard · Passivhaus · Net Zero Carbon
- ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 — name them, know what they are

## Contract Forms (Awareness Level)

- JCT — Standard Building Contract, Design & Build, Minor Works (know they exist; understand JCT = risk-allocating)
- NEC — NEC4 (most common in current civils/infrastructure; understand NEC = collaborative, programme-driven)
- Don't claim to know contract clauses or to have administered contracts unless you have

## Methodologies & Processes

- Design & Build (D&B) · Two-stage tender / Single-stage tender · Pre-Construction Services Agreement (PCSA) · Early Contractor Involvement (ECI)
- 4D / 5D BIM · Soft landings · Snag-free handover · Defects period
- Value engineering · Buildability review · Risk management
- Modern Methods of Construction (MMC) · Modular / Volumetric / Offsite manufacture

## Software & Systems (Be Honest About Level)

**Programme:**
- MS Project — most students use this in coursework
- Asta Powerproject — less common in unis; if you've used it, say so
- Primavera P6 — typically professional software; only claim if you've actually used it

**Design & BIM:**
- AutoCAD — basic / intermediate / advanced (be honest)
- Revit — basic / intermediate (most uni students reach basic-intermediate)
- Navisworks — claim only if you've actually used it
- BIM 360 / Autodesk Construction Cloud
- SketchUp

**Site Management (Less Common in Uni):**
- Procore · Aconex · Fieldview · PlanRadar · SnagR · Bluebeam Revu

**Common Office:**
- Microsoft Excel — be specific about level (basic, intermediate with PivotTables, advanced with INDEX/MATCH and VBA)
- Word · PowerPoint · Outlook · Teams · SharePoint

## Trades & Packages (Background Knowledge)

Have basic vocabulary even if you haven't worked with them: Façades / Curtain walling / Rainscreen · MEP / M&E · Fit-out (CAT A, CAT B) · Drylining · Joinery · Brickwork / Blockwork · RC frame / Steel frame · Roofing · Glazing · Flooring · Decoration · Groundworks · Piling

## Sectors (Know What Each Means)

- Residential — private sale, BTR (Build-to-Rent), PRS (Private Rented Sector), social, student accommodation, co-living
- Commercial offices — Cat A (landlord shell), Cat B (tenant fit-out)
- Mixed-use
- Hotel / Hospitality
- Retail
- Education — primary, secondary, FE (Further Education), HE (Higher Education)
- Healthcare — primary care, acute, mental health
- Listed buildings / Conservation / Heritage
- High-rise / HRB (Higher-Risk Buildings under BSA 2022)
- Industrial / Logistics / Data centres
- Civils & Infrastructure (rail, highways, utilities) — typically civil engineering territory rather than CM, but relevant for civils-leaning grad schemes

## Soft / Behavioural Keywords (Often in Grad JDs)

These appear constantly in graduate JDs and ATS systems pick them up:
- Leadership / leadership potential
- Teamwork / collaborative
- Communication (written and verbal)
- Problem-solving / analytical thinking
- Attention to detail
- Time management / organisation
- Initiative / self-starter
- Resilience / adaptability
- Customer focus / stakeholder awareness
- Commercial awareness
- Continuous learning / growth mindset
- Integrity / professionalism

When tailoring a CV, look at the JD's "About You" or "What We're Looking For" section and weave the soft skills it names into your bullets — provided you have evidence.

## Hard Mismatches (Real Reasons to Skip a Job)

If a JD contains these and the student doesn't match, skip the application:
- "We do not offer visa sponsorship" + student needs sponsorship
- "Minimum 2:1 expected" + student's predicted grade is below
- "Civil Engineering degree only" + student is on Construction Management
- "Year-in-industry placement" + student is final year (you've missed the window)
- "Final-year student / recent graduate" + student is Year 1 or 2
