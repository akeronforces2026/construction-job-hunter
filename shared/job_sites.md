# Job Sites — Per-Platform Guide for Students

Eight platforms, in priority order for UK construction students.

## 1. Prospects (prospects.ac.uk)

**Why first:** UK's main graduate jobs platform. Covers grad schemes, placements, and internships. Filtered well by sector and location.

- Graduate jobs: `https://www.prospects.ac.uk/graduate-jobs`
- Placement jobs: `https://www.prospects.ac.uk/internships`
- Search by job sector: select "Property and construction"

**Tip:** Set up email alerts. Many tier 1 contractors only post their grad scheme here and on their own careers pages.

## 2. TARGETjobs (targetjobs.co.uk)

**Why:** Graduate-focused with detailed employer profiles, salary information, and assessment process descriptions. Excellent for researching companies before applying.

- Construction: `https://targetjobs.co.uk/sectors/construction-and-building-services`
- Placements: `https://targetjobs.co.uk/internships`

**Tip:** Their "Inside Buzz" reviews give you real insight into what employees say about each firm.

## 3. RateMyPlacement (ratemyplacement.co.uk)

**Why:** Best site for placements (year-in-industry). Has student reviews of every employer — invaluable when deciding between offers.

- Placements: `https://www.ratemyplacement.co.uk/search?type=placement&industry=construction`
- Internships: `https://www.ratemyplacement.co.uk/search?type=internship&industry=construction`

**Tip:** Read the reviews from previous placement students before applying. Salary, work-life balance, and training quality vary massively.

## 4. LinkedIn

**Why:** Increasingly important even for grad roles. Direct connection with grad recruiters works.

- Grad scheme search: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=construction%20graduate%20scheme&location=United%20Kingdom&f_TPR=r2592000&f_E=1`
- Placement search: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?keywords=construction%20placement&location=United%20Kingdom&f_TPR=r2592000`

`f_E=1` = Internship/Entry-level. `f_TPR=r2592000` = past 30 days.

**Tip:** Connect with the graduate recruiter mentioned on the JD before submitting your application. Politely ask one specific question — it gets your name in front of them.

## 5. Indeed UK

**Why:** Highest volume; many regional and SME contractors only post here.

- Grad: `https://uk.indeed.com/jobs?q=construction+graduate&l=United+Kingdom&fromage=14`
- Placement: `https://uk.indeed.com/jobs?q=construction+placement&l=United+Kingdom&fromage=14`

**Tip:** Indeed deduplicates poorly. The same role often appears via different agencies — Phase 2.1 dup check catches this.

## 6. Handshake

**Why:** University-affiliated platform. Good if your university uses it (check with your Careers Service). Some UK universities are on Handshake — many aren't.

- URL: `https://app.joinhandshake.co.uk/job-search/`

**Honest note:** UK construction depth on Handshake is variable. Use it but don't rely on it as a primary source.

## 7. CareersinConstruction (careersinconstruction.com)

**Why:** UK construction-only specialist. Has a dedicated graduate section and many tier 2 / regional contractor postings.

- Graduate: `https://www.careersinconstruction.com/jobs/graduate/`
- Search by location once you've filtered by graduate.

**Tip:** Sign up for the daily email digest — covers regional contractors that don't advertise widely elsewhere.

## 8. University Careers Service

**Why:** Often has exclusive postings from local contractors looking specifically for students from your university. Tier 1 firms also post grad schemes here as part of their university partnerships.

- Visit your uni's careers portal (every UK uni has one — usually at careers.youruni.ac.uk or jobs.youruni.ac.uk)
- Book a 1:1 with a careers advisor — most universities offer this free for students and recent graduates

**Tip:** Underused by students. Local SME contractors often prefer to recruit from a single uni they have a relationship with, so the competition is much lower than on Prospects or LinkedIn.

---

## Cross-Site Hygiene

- The same grad scheme often appears on 3–5 sites. Phase 2.1 catches duplicates by URL.
- For placements, **apply early.** Many tier 1 placement schemes open in September/October for the following summer and close as soon as they're full, regardless of stated deadline.
- For grad schemes, the application window is typically **September to December** of your final year, with assessment centres running through to spring.
- Keep an eye on **employer-specific careers pages** for the largest tier 1s (Mace, Skanska, Sir Robert McAlpine, Bouygues, Multiplex, Wates, Balfour Beatty, Kier, Galliford Try, Morgan Sindall, BAM Construct, Vinci, Laing O'Rourke, Costain, Willmott Dixon) — they sometimes open their schemes there before posting elsewhere.
