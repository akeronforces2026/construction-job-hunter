# Fit Score Rubric — Student Edition

Score every job 0–100 across six categories. Show the student the breakdown, not just the total.

## Category 1 — Role Match (25 pts)

| Condition | Points |
|---|---|
| JD title is "Graduate Construction Manager" / "Trainee Construction Manager" / "Construction Management Graduate Scheme" exactly | 25 |
| JD title is "Year-in-Industry Placement" / "Construction Placement" and matches student's year level | 25 |
| JD title is "Summer Internship" / "Industrial Placement" — Construction | 22 |
| JD title is "Graduate Site Manager" / "Assistant Site Manager (Graduate)" | 22 |
| JD title is "Trainee Project Manager" (Construction) | 18 |
| JD title is "Apprentice Construction Manager" (Level 6) | 15 (only relevant if student is open to switching) |
| JD title is "Graduate Quantity Surveyor" / "Graduate Engineer" | 5 (note: this skill targets CM, not QS or engineering) |
| JD title is a senior role inappropriately listed | 0 — flag |

## Category 2 — Course Relevance (20 pts)

| Course on student's CV vs JD's preferred discipline | Points |
|---|---|
| JD names student's exact course (e.g., "BSc Construction Management") | 20 |
| JD names a related discipline student is in (Civil Eng, Building Surveying, Architectural Tech) | 17 |
| JD says "construction or related" / "any STEM degree" | 14 |
| JD doesn't specify a course | 12 |
| JD names a discipline student is NOT in (e.g., "Quantity Surveying degree only") | 0 — flag as a hard mismatch |

## Category 3 — Year Level Fit (15 pts)

| Match | Points |
|---|---|
| Placement role + student is Year 3 (placement year) | 15 |
| Grad scheme + student is final year or recent grad | 15 |
| Summer internship + student is any year | 15 |
| Grad scheme + student is Year 3 (some firms accept early applications for the year after) | 8 — flag as a stretch |
| Trainee role (post-grad) + student is final year | 12 |
| Wrong year level entirely (e.g., grad scheme but student is Year 2) | 0 — flag |

## Category 4 — Soft Skills + Extracurriculars (15 pts)

JDs for grad / placement roles emphasise soft skills heavily because students don't have professional experience yet. Score:

- 4–5 of the soft skills the JD names are clearly evidenced in the CV (with examples): 15
- 2–3 evidenced: 10
- 1 evidenced: 5
- None evidenced: 0

Common soft skills in construction grad JDs: leadership, teamwork, communication, problem-solving, analytical, attention to detail, time management, initiative, resilience, customer focus.

## Category 5 — Hard Requirements (15 pts)

For students, hard requirements are usually short:
- Right to work (with or without sponsorship)
- Driving licence (for site-based roles)
- Expected grade (often 2:1 minimum)
- CSCS card (for roles involving site visits)

| Condition | Points |
|---|---|
| All listed hard requirements met | 15 |
| All but one met (and that one is reasonable to acquire — CSCS, driving lessons booked) | 11 |
| Two missing | 6 |
| Right-to-work mismatch (student needs sponsorship, employer doesn't sponsor) | 0 — flag, do not apply |

## Category 6 — Location (10 pts)

| Match against student's location preferences from Phase -1 | Points |
|---|---|
| Within student's stated preferred location(s) | 10 |
| Within commute radius (if student set one) | 8 |
| UK-wide and student is willing to relocate | 7 |
| Outside student's preferred area but UK | 3 — flag |
| Outside UK | 0 — flag |

## Bands

- **85–100:** Excellent. Apply within 24–48 hours (grad schemes can fill fast).
- **70–84:** Strong. Apply this week.
- **55–69:** Worth applying. Tailor carefully and address gaps in cover letter.
- **40–54:** Stretch. Only apply if student really wants this employer.
- **<40:** Skip unless there's a specific reason (e.g., the contractor is a target).

## Output Format

```
FIT SCORE: 76/100 — Strong match

  Role match:                 22/25  (Graduate Construction Manager — direct match)
  Course relevance:           17/20  (JD prefers Civil Eng; you're Construction Mgmt — close)
  Year level fit:             15/15  (Final-year, applying for grad scheme)
  Soft skills + extras:       12/15  (Leadership ✓, teamwork ✓, communication ✓; less evidence of "analytical")
  Hard requirements:          10/15  (You don't have a driving licence yet — JD says "preferred, not essential")
  Location:                    0/10  (Edinburgh — outside your preferred areas of London/SE)

Recommendation: Strong on fit but location is a real issue. Worth applying only if you'd genuinely relocate to Edinburgh. Address the licence gap in cover letter (if you're working towards it).
```

## Important Student-Specific Calibration

For students, **do NOT penalise heavily** for these because they're commonly trainable:
- SMSTS / SSSTS / NEBOSH (employer will provide)
- Specific software (AutoCAD, Revit, MS Project, Asta) — they'll train you
- "5+ years' experience" on a grad role (this is usually a copy-paste mistake)
- "Chartered status" on a grad scheme (they mean working-towards)

**Do** flag these as real concerns:
- No CSCS card AND the role is heavily site-based (Get the £36 green card before applying)
- Sponsorship needed AND the employer's careers page says "We do not sponsor visas"
- Wrong year level (applying for grad scheme as a Year 2)
- Predicted grade below the minimum stated
