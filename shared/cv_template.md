## STAR Bullet Pattern for Students

> **[Strong action verb]** [task/scope] **on/at/for** [situation/project], [specific actions taken]; **delivering [result, quantified where possible]**.

For students, Results don't have to be £-figures. Valid Results include:
- Module / project grades (e.g., "achieved 78%, the highest in our cohort")
- Team outcomes ("our team won the inter-university design competition")
- Personal recognition ("nominated for the Department's leadership award")
- Quantified outputs ("led 6 volunteers", "organised an event for 120 attendees", "raised £2,400 for charity")
- Process improvements ("reduced our group's coordination meetings from 4 to 2 per week by introducing a shared task board")

**Example 3 — Part-time retail / hospitality work (transferable skills)**

> **[REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN]** Worked weekends and holidays for two years at [retailer], training three new staff members on the till and stock procedures, dealing with up to 200 customers per shift; **promoted to shift supervisor in my second year, evidencing time management, communication, and team leadership under pressure**.

**Example 4 — Society leadership**

> **[REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN]** Elected Chair of the [University] CIOB Student Chapter for 2024–25, leading a committee of 6 to plan and run a year-long programme of 8 industry talks and site visits; **grew membership by 35% and secured sponsorship from two regional contractors**.

**Example 5 — Volunteering**

> **[REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN]** Volunteered with [charity / community group] on a weekend self-build project, coordinating tool inventory and inducting 4 new volunteers per session over a 6-month period; **contributed to the completion of a community garden delivered on time and within a £8,000 donated-materials budget**.

### Verbs to Use (Student-Friendly)

- **Project work:** led, coordinated, designed, analysed, modelled, presented, researched, evaluated, delivered, contributed
- **Team:** mentored, trained, briefed, organised, scheduled, motivated
- **Site / placement:** shadowed (entry-level OK), supported, observed, documented, drafted, assisted
- **Soft skills evidence:** communicated, negotiated, resolved, adapted, prioritised
- **Achievements:** achieved, won, secured, completed, delivered, exceeded

## What NOT to Do (Common Student CV Mistakes)

- ❌ "Responsible for..." — passive, no Result. Always lead with action verb + Result.
- ❌ Listing every module — pick only those relevant to the JD.
- ❌ Saying "Microsoft Office" without specifics — say "Excel (Advanced — INDEX/MATCH, pivot tables, basic VBA)" or just "Excel" and not list the rest.
- ❌ Hobbies that say nothing ("reading, watching films, going to the gym") — either be specific or omit.
- ❌ A photo, marital status, date of birth, or full home address — UK CVs don't include any of these.
- ❌ More than two pages for an undergraduate. One page is fine for Years 1–2.
- ❌ Inventing experience or grades. Every UK construction firm checks references and grade transcripts before offer.
- ❌ Using "I" — bullets are subject-implied.
